Privacy policy

Selomira is a private, single-user application. Its only user is the owner of the connected Google account.

  • It accesses Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets) only of its owner's own account and only to help that owner with their own tasks.
  • Data stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
